PRISMS-PF: Laplace's Equation

This example application solves Laplace's equation with Dirichlet boundary values.

$$ \begin{align} \nabla\cdot\nabla u_{\Omega} = 0 \ u_{\partial \Omega} = \Gamma_D \end{align} $$

where $\Gamma_D$ are the Dirichlet boundary values.

Governing equations

In the weak form (and multiplying by -1), we have

$$ \begin{align} \int_{\Omega} \nabla w \cdot \nabla u ~dV = \int_{\Omega}0~dV \end{align} $$

Expressions

In PRISMS-PF, linear equations are solved provided expressions for the left and right hand side of equations of the form $Ax=b$. From the weak form, we find

$$ \begin{align} r_{ux}^{LHS} = \nabla u^* \end{align} $$

$$ \begin{align} r_{u}^{RHS} = 0 \end{align} $$

The ${}^*$ indicates a trial solution.

The above equations are used to define expressions in the file: applications/laplace/custom_pde.h
